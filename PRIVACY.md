# Privacy policy

Enhance ProgArchives Forum, as a Chrome, Firefox or Safari extension, as the Safari app that carries it, or
as a userscript, collects no data at all.

- It runs only on the pages of the ProgArchives.com forum (`https://www.progarchives.com/forum/`), where it
  restyles the page and adds a theme switch.
- It sends nothing anywhere: it makes no network requests of its own and contains no analytics, tracking or
  advertising.
- The one thing it stores is the theme you choose (Auto, Dark or Light), in the forum's own `localStorage`
  in your browser. It never leaves your device, and clearing the site's data or removing the extension
  removes it.
- The Safari app shows instructions for turning the extension on, and nothing else. It collects nothing
  either.

Enhance ProgArchives Forum is not made by or affiliated with ProgArchives. Questions:
https://github.com/MikeEnRegalia/enhance-pa/issues
