**Description:**

> Enhance ProgArchives Forum is a Safari extension that gives the ProgArchives.com forum a dark mode and a
> more readable style.
>
> DARK, LIGHT OR AUTOMATIC
> The theme follows your device's setting, or you choose: the Theme link in the forum's own menu goes round
> Auto, Dark and Light, and remembers your choice. The dark page never flashes white while it loads.
>
> MEMBERS' COLOURS STAY READABLE
> Text a member coloured too dark to read on a dark background is lightened, keeping its hue, and put back
> when you switch to light.
>
> EASIER TO READ IN ANY THEME
> Slightly larger text with more line spacing, no tall empty boxes around one-line posts, and images, long
> words and quotes kept inside the post.
>
> ON IPHONE, IPAD AND MAC
> The forum's pages for phones get the same dark theme, with the switch beside the links at the top.
>
> After installing, open the app for the few steps that turn the extension on in Safari.
>
> No account, no data collected: the extension runs only on the forum's pages and sends nothing anywhere.
> It is open source, under the Apache License 2.0.
>
> Enhance ProgArchives Forum is not made by or affiliated with ProgArchives.

## Notes for App Review

> This app carries a Safari web extension, and its own screen only explains how to turn the extension on.
>
> To try it: turn on "Enhance ProgArchives Forum" in Settings › Apps › Safari › Extensions (on a Mac: Safari ›
> Settings › Extensions, or the app's "Open Safari Settings…" button) and allow it on www.progarchives.com.
> Then open a forum thread in Safari, for example
> https://www.progarchives.com/forum/forum_posts.asp?TID=137744. The page follows the device's light or dark
> setting, and the "Theme" link in the forum's menu switches between Auto, Dark and Light.
>
> No account is needed and the extension collects no data. It is not affiliated with ProgArchives; its name
> says what it works on, and the listing and the app both say it is unofficial.
